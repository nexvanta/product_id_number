Product ID Number

Overview

Product ID Number is a powerful Odoo extension that introduces a dedicated unique ID field for products and ensures its visibility across Purchase, Sales, Inventory, and PDF reports.

Descriptions

Product ID Number is a practical and powerful Odoo extension that adds a dedicated unique Product ID field to your product records and ensures its visibility across major business workflows. Many organizations require a structured or numeric product identification system beyond the default internal reference, especially for compliance, reporting accuracy, warehouse control, and audit clarity. This module introduces a custom Product ID Number field in the product form and automatically integrates it into Purchase, Sales, and Inventory operations.

Key Features

  1. Adds dedicated Product ID Number field
  2. Visible in Purchase RFQ & Orders
  3. Appears in Vendor Bills
  4. Shown in Sales Quotations & Orders
  5. Visible in Customer Invoices
  6. Integrated into Sales & Purchase PDF Reports
  7. Available in Inventory Location Reports
  8. Lightweight and performance-safe
  9. No core modification
  10. Easy installation
