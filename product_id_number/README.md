# product_id_number

Streamline your product identification in Odoo. This module adds a dedicated Custom ID field to your products, ensuring better tracking and organization across your inventory.

## 🚀 Features
* **Product Custom ID:** Adds a unique identification field to the product form for better internal tracking.
* **Auto-Sync Variants:** Automatically synchronizes the Custom ID from Product Templates to all related Product Variants.
* **Stored Data:** The field is stored in the database, making it fully searchable and filterable in list views.
* **Standard Integration:** Works perfectly with Odoo's native Inventory, Sales, and Purchase workflows.

## 🛠 Installation
1. Copy the `product_id_number` folder to your Odoo custom addons directory.
2. Restart your Odoo server.
3. Enable developer mode and go to **Apps > Update Apps List**.
4. Search for **"product_id_number"** and click **Install**.
